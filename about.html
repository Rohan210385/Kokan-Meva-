About KokanMewa

Bringing the Traditional Taste of Konkan to Your Home!

Welcome to KokanMewa

KokanMewa is an online shopping platform that brings traditional Konkan food products and authentic flavours to customers. Our aim is to make shopping for delicious regional products easy and convenient.

Who We Are

KokanMewa is an ecommerce website focused on traditional Konkan products. We provide a platform where customers can explore and purchase different food products in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to introduce the traditional taste of Konkan to more people through a simple, user-friendly and convenient online shopping experience.

Our Products

We offer products such as cashews, Malvani kaju, modak flour, ghavan flour, vada flour, mango pulp, pickles, kokam juice, amla squash, jackfruit chips and traditional fruit poli.

Why Choose KokanMewa?

Customers can explore different products, view product details, check prices and add their favourite products to the shopping cart. Our website makes online shopping simple.

Our Promise

Celebrating the taste, tradition and flavours of Konkan with KokanMewa!
